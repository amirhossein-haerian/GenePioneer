## Methodology & Interpretation Guide

### Statistical Methods
- **Enrichment Analysis:** Hypergeometric test comparing module genes to cancer hallmark pathways
- **P-value:** Probability that the observed overlap occurred by chance
- **Enrichment Ratio:** Fold-enrichment compared to random expectation
- **Background:** 20,000 genes used as genomic background

### Significance Levels
- **p < 0.001:** Highly significant (very strong evidence)
- **p < 0.01:** Very significant (strong evidence)
- **p < 0.05:** Significant (meaningful evidence)

### Enrichment Interpretation
- **>10x enrichment:** Very strong functional relationship
- **5-10x enrichment:** Strong functional relationship
- **2-5x enrichment:** Moderate functional relationship
- **1-2x enrichment:** Weak functional relationship

### Cancer Hallmark Pathways
These represent fundamental cancer processes identified by Hanahan & Weinberg:
